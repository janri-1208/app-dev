# My Favorite Series

## TV Show/Series: **Brooklyn Nine-Nine**
One of my all-time favorite shows is **Brooklyn Nine-Nine**. Peak humor and best to watch if you are looking for a series with a cozy and fun vibes.

**Favorite Characters**:
    - Jake Peralta
    - Amy Santiago
    - Rosa Diaz
    - Captain Holt
    - Terry Jeffords
    - Gina Linetti
    - Charles Boyle

### Why I Love It
- **Humor**: The jokes and wit in this show are hilarious and light-hearted, making it easy to watch any time.
- **Diverse Characters**: The diverse cast brings different perspectives and adds to the overall charm of the show.
- **Great Chemistry**: The interactions between the characters, especially Jake and Holt, are iconic and make the show truly memorable.
- **Heartfelt Moments**: Though primarily a comedy, it has touching moments that really make you care about the characters.

### Kewl facts about Brooklyn Nine-Nine
- The show has earned numerous awards, including Golden Globe Awards for Best Actor in a Television Series – Musical or Comedy for Andy Samberg (Jake Peralta).
- **Catchphrases**: "Noice," "Cool, cool, cool, cool, cool," and "Jake, why are you like this?" are some of the best lines and indeed funny xD.
